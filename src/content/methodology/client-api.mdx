On the client API layer, decentralisation is evaluated based on the distribution of traffic (e.g. web traffic and API calls) across RPC providers. Although this metric is not directly measured, it is assumed that a significant proportion of blockchain transactions are routed through these APIs. Unlike the other layers, the analysis is not ledger-specific, as most RPC providers offer services for multiple ledgers.

The analysis uses data from the <AppLink href="https://tranco-list.eu/">Tranco</AppLink> project, which provides rankings of the one million most popular websites. The domains associated with RPC providers are identified, and their respective rankings are converted into estimated shares of RPC traffic using Zipf's law with an exponent of 1. This approach is supported by two research papers: <AppLink href="https://doi.org/10.1016/j.econlet.2015.01.019">The Size Distribution of Websites</AppLink> by Schmeiser S. and <AppLink href="https://doi.org/10.1016/j.comnet.2005.07.009">On The Universality of Rank Distributions of Website Popularity</AppLink> by Krashakov S. A. et al.

The dashboard displays the ten providers with the highest average estimated traffic shares individually, while aggregating all remaining providers into a single category labelled "Others".
